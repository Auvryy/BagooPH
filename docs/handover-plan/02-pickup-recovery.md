# H02 — Seller and rider recovery

Start after H01 review and publication. Cover parcels handed to a pickup rider before readiness was recorded and assigned riders missing a pickup or destination-hub departure scan.

## Approach

1. Classify the discrepancy from retained original facts and current physical evidence. Unknown possession remains unresolved; do not guess a rider or create an assignment retrospectively.
2. Introduce a reviewed operational hold across relevant seller, rider, hub and native mutation services. A hold prevents incompatible movement, reassignment and duplicate delivery outcomes. Read access and evidence review remain available. Identify every writer before choosing the guard placement.
3. Require independent authorization plus the actual holder's acknowledgement and an eligible receiving party's matching-waybill receipt. Handle inactive holders using the existing restricted-custody principles, without reopening general portal access.
4. Append a corrective custody event with the actual server recording time and explicit original source. Treat a claimed occurrence time as an observation, never as the scan timestamp. Keep original readiness and commercial history honest.
5. Resume only a valid canonical route and commercial state. Preserve COD, stock and buyer completion ownership. Conflicting, expired, withdrawn and retried authorizations must have safe outcomes.

## Acceptance gate

Verify both sides of the handover, authority loss, exact waybill, source changes, expiry, cancellation, repeated/competing receipt, rollback and every held mutation through web and native services. Preserve the original checkpoints and evidence. Do not broaden to manifest repair or claim all missed scans are supported.
