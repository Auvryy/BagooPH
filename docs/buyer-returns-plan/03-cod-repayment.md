# R03: Original money, recovery and verified repayment

Follow the [shared contract](README.md). A decision and a physical receipt do not
move money. Complete the financial prerequisites before labelling a case repaid.

## Exact original amount

Compute original merchandise cents, gross commission and original product-
discount funding by exact purchased item/unit positions. Use deterministic
allocation in original item order, retain rounding remainders and prove that all
units sum to the original amount. Never use today's listing price or let a
client choose entitlement, commission or payee.

For returned units retain:

- Gross original merchandise and its allocated original 10% commission.
- Original shop-funded and platform-funded **product** discounts.
- Buyer repayment: gross merchandise less those original product discounts.
- Seller contribution: original allocated seller share less shop product funding.
- Platform contribution: original allocated commission less platform product
  funding. This may be signed for a heavily platform-funded original purchase;
  it is never coerced into a fabricated nonnegative cash amount.

Seller and platform contributions sum exactly to buyer merchandise repayment.
Original shipping, its discounts and sibling orders remain separate and unchanged.
Refunding returned merchandise does not restore original voucher use or campaign
budget automatically. Retain original funding records and separately describe
return corrections; no double release/expense or deletion of redemption history.

## Before seller payment

An unresolved case holds release of the affected original order. Once a return
is approved for repayment after its actual receipt/condition review, append the
fixed return adjustment and derive remaining seller proceeds from the original
funded amount. Preserve original authorized and gross ledger evidence.

A changed return basis after an earlier authorization requires explicit renewed
admin authorization of the actual payable amount and linked adjustments. Do not
silently reuse the old payment consent, overwrite immutable settlement fields or
make a historical paid event become unpaid. A genuinely zero-payable remainder
uses a separate evidenced closure, not an invented zero-cash transfer.

Existing seller settlement still needs buyer completion, exact original COD
collection and platform reconciliation. After return deductions its payment and
funding evidence must remain consistent. Completed return cases cannot release
money that an unresolved cash difference or another case still holds.

## After seller payment

Retain the original paid settlement and proof. The returned units create their
exact seller recovery liability, tied to that payment and the immutable return
adjustment. Seller recovery is an offered/received cash record with source,
amount, reference, proof and distinct owner/recipient authority. A pending offer
is not counted as cash received. Partial or missing recovery remains a visible
outstanding debt and cannot be erased by closing or suspending an account.

Determine actual available backing from original reconciled money, retained
seller payment and confirmed recovery, while keeping shipping obligations and
other return allocations separate. If repayment needs recovery first, report
that state honestly. Do not invent an unfunded platform advance or pretend a
seller-debt label replenishes cash. Independently record each received recovery
and prevent overcollection or reuse against a different case. The transfer offer
names the current receiving platform admin; that distinct named account records
its own receipt and private evidence. A restricted original seller may satisfy
this existing recovery duty without gaining selling access.

If original promotion costs leave insufficient backing, a current admin may
record an actual platform funding transfer to a different current admin. The
named recipient must independently confirm receipt. Require an exact positive
amount, unique normalized reference, actual private proof and explicit transfer
attestation on both sides. Pending offers and original campaign budgets never
increase available cash. This is manual evidence of an actual transfer; no bank
integration or automatic advance is introduced.

## Outgoing repayment and buyer receipt

Only a current authorized admin may approve/record an exact eligible repayment.
Require original confirmed COD source, resolved return condition, actual custody
receipt and available backing. Bind the payment to the original buyer and fixed
entitlement, a meaningful reference, private content-verified proof and explicit
confirmation that the operator made the manual payment.

Retain a payment-recorded state while awaiting the buyer's own receipt
confirmation. The authenticated original buyer confirms the referenced payment;
only then is the case repaid/closed. A buyer reporting non-receipt leaves an
actual review/discrepancy and never authorizes a second payout automatically.
A fully product-discounted returned item may have zero buyer entitlement. Close
that case with an explicit no-repayment-due decision rather than inventing a
zero-money transfer or buyer payment receipt.
No bank API, online gateway, wallet balance or external provider is introduced.

Idempotency, unique payment/reference/source constraints, order/case locks and
immutable journals prevent repeated or cross-case repayment. Failed proof,
notice, audit or write operations roll back together and remove only new
unreferenced uploads. Corrections append to original evidence instead of changing
amount, recipient, past time or actual payment identity.

## Views, restrictions and acceptance

Buyer screens distinguish requested, approved, physically returned, payment
recorded and receipt confirmed. Sellers see original proceeds, return deductions,
actual payment, recovery offered/received and outstanding liability. Admin finance
shows original collection/remittance and separate returns/outgoing money; historical
cash metrics are not relabelled as available balances. Use exact cents and clear
human explanations rather than a guessed order count or gross amount.

Recheck every role's current authority and source ownership. Buyer proof access is limited to their actual repayment and payment-review
evidence; internal platform funding is visible only to current admins. Logistics
operators see operational return history without financial notes or proofs.
Restricted owners
may satisfy their narrowly scoped existing cash/receipt responsibilities and read
owned history, without new shopping, selling or administrative authority. Closure
is blocked while cases, custody, repayment or seller recovery remain outstanding.

Verify full and partial quantity/amount returns before authorization, after
unpaid authorization and after paid seller settlement; zero remaining proceeds;
stacked product/shipping offers; signed platform contributions; exact allocation
remainders; multiple cases and sibling orders; received vs offered recovery;
insufficient backing; foreign/stale/replayed/conflicting writes; private proof
integrity; rollback; once-only outgoing cash; buyer confirmation/dispute; original
ledger retention and truthful finance totals. Include a genuine complete cross-
role return/repayment run and the related existing financial regression suites.

Isolated local evidence and builds do not establish a real production repayment
or a bank transfer. Final handoff states those limits and the actual release state.
