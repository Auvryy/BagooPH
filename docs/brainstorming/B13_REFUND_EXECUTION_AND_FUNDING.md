# B13: evidenced refunds and actual funding

This contract implements the selected [B04 money decisions](B04_MONEY_AND_LIABILITY.md)
after [B11 review](B11_RETURN_REQUESTS_AND_REVIEW.md) and
[B12 physical custody](B12_REVERSE_TRANSPORT_AND_SHARED_TRIPS.md). Purchased
terms remain versioned. Current implementation, activation and verification
evidence belong in [the roadmap](../CORE_FLOW_ROADMAP.md).

## Original entitlement and independent approval

An approval preserves the exact original unit claims, net merchandise,
buyer-paid shipping allocation, payment evidence, completed physical return
and original fault decision. Seller recovery is frozen against the actual
seller payout at that approval. A later payment of remaining seller proceeds
does not turn those remaining units into newly refunded seller debt.

The original merchandise adjustment keeps its merchandise-only fields.
The successor plan separately records total buyer refund, allocated paid
shipping, seller/commission/promotion reversals, liable-company funding and
protected original shipping. Courier fault does not reduce original seller
proceeds or create duplicate seller protection. A decision or zero-money plan
approval cannot count as a transfer, received backing or buyer repayment.

Paid shipping is allocated over stable original units with cumulative integer
arithmetic. Original net merchandise supplies the weights; original gross
weights apply when all net merchandise is zero. Shipping-voucher subsidy is
excluded from the buyer-paid amount. Partial claims together retain every
original cent without refunding a unit twice.

## Original COD and actual cash transfers

Original COD must have its actual collection and platform reconciliation.
An offer records the named actual source, positive bounded amount, unique
transfer reference, private real receipt and distinct receiving admin.
Only that receiving admin can acknowledge the original offer once. A pending
offer and an outstanding debt are separate from money actually received.

Seller fault requires the approved seller recovery or a separately evidenced
platform advance. Courier fault requires its approved company compensation or
an evidenced advance. The plan's required case funding and the order's actual
cash backing must both hold before repayment. Protect original gross shipping,
remaining seller proceeds, prior seller payments and already recorded buyer
repayments. Received company money cannot silently substitute for original
unreconciled COD.

Repayment retains one original buyer, amount, private proof and transfer
reference. The buyer separately confirms receipt or reports non-receipt.
Reviewing that report preserves the original payment and adds evidence;
it cannot record a second repayment. A genuinely zero entitlement closes
without pretending a transfer occurred. An advance does not clear the original
liable party's debt. A restricted original liable account can still settle
its existing obligation through its authorized financial access.

## Balanced virtual reserves

Wallet funding retains actual balanced ledger entries. A request, approval,
promised recovery, cash receipt or assumed insurer deposit cannot create
virtual backing. User balances and the refund reserve cannot go negative.
An explicitly approved demonstration advance uses the retained demonstration
issuance journal, is labelled virtual and remains separate from liable-party
recovery. It is not a cash payment, bank refund or insurance fund.

An original liable seller or company without sufficient credits may request
a bounded demonstration grant. A separate current admin reviews the original
request and its remaining need before issuing credits to that account's own
Wallet. Approval retains the exact grant journal and final review. It does not
fund the refund reserve or clear liability: the account separately transfers
its own available credits into the reserve. Pending requests block account
closure; rejection moves no credits. Repeated requests cannot exceed the
original obligation or duplicate an outstanding request.

Reviewed liability credits remain available to their original financial
authority until that account actually contributes them or their original
source is independently reconciled. Other unrestricted owned credits may
fund another approved obligation. Availability follows the retained grant
and owned-contribution journal order: a contribution made before a grant
cannot consume that later grant. Another authority's reserved grant does
not reduce this authority's independently reviewed shortfall. Original
grant limits and the actual owned Wallet balance limit still apply.

Each authority has its own reserve entries linked to the exact actual Wallet
transaction, reserve account and signed journal delta. Funding one case cannot
finance another case's refund. The named liable user actually transfers their
own credits once; refund execution consumes that reserve and does not debit
the seller or company again.

| Original post-delivery finding | Required original reserve | Additional liable funding | Actual reserve use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seller fault before original settlement | Returned net item amount from the original unspent escrow | Allocated buyer-paid shipping | Original buyer refund; original unspent promotion remains separate |
| Seller fault after original settlement | Returned original commission | Returned seller share plus allocated buyer-paid shipping | Buyer refund plus the original spent product-promotion contribution |
| Courier fault after paid delivery | No reversal of original seller, commission or promotion proceeds | Full approved buyer entitlement | Original buyer refund; no second seller protection credit |

The spent product-promotion contribution can exceed the returned commission.
Preserve its signed allocation and fund the complete balanced reserve use.
Restoring a financial promotion account does not restore campaign budget,
redemption availability or shipping-voucher funding automatically.

Record the actual original escrow debit separately from the total buyer
refund. Externally funded shipping or courier compensation cannot reduce
original escrow a second time. Remaining settlement consumers use explicit
approved reversals and actual original source debits. Earned shipping stays
in its retained pool.

Actual liable credits received after an advance remain a separate receipt.
Only excess reserve beyond the unpaid buyer/promotion obligation can retire
the original demonstration advance. Retirement is another balanced retained
journal, bounded by the actual outstanding advance. It cannot charge the user
again, increase the buyer refund or consume another authority's reserve.

## Loss and conforming buyer refusal

A manual loss review requires original custody/exception evidence and an
independent decision naming the liable company. A timeout or last scan alone
cannot prove loss. Preserve actual buyer payments, original proceeds and
eligible seller protection separately. Uncollected COD has zero buyer repayment;
seller protection cannot duplicate original seller proceeds. No fictitious
reverse arrival or seller receipt can supply the missing loss evidence.

An independent current admin can account for a confirmed lost parcel only
against its retained original approval, operational disposition and private
evidence. This is a separate zero-money record, not a parcel receipt. Preserve
the exact forward or buyer-return member identity, including the earlier
original member and observation when the parcel was lost during recovery.
Report confirmed losses separately from normal and corrective receipts.
Manifest accounting can close only when every included member has an actual
supported receipt or this independently supported loss accounting, with no
other unresolved discrepancy or supervised handover hold.

Closing accounting does not mark the parcel received, finish its route, change
its last actual custody, move money or prove vehicle arrival. An actual bag
opening can finish that bag once all its members are independently accounted
for, without giving a missing member a fictitious receipt. Retain the original
driver, vehicle and unarrived sack reservations until their own physical
arrival or independent transport accountability is established. New grants,
funding and payments still require the original retained evidence after
compensation has been paid. A later finding preserves supported historical
loss accounting while adding its separate physical and financial hold.

An approved loss cannot resume through ordinary manifest receiving, hub scans
or unexpected-hub recovery. An actual later finding uses the exact original
typed waybill, an assigned handler at a facility of the original company,
bounded private evidence and explicit physical quarantine confirmation.
Retain the original missing member, route position, sale, payments and
transport/sack accountability. Record the actual quarantine holder separately;
do not manufacture an original manifest receipt, vehicle arrival or route
advance. An unfunded financial void alone cannot release quarantined property.

The finding holds new grants, funding, compensation and financial resolution,
including after an advance-backed refund was already paid. Exact successful
financial retries retain their original references. Named receipts for actual
transfers already offered, and confirmation or nonreceipt review of actual
beneficiary payments already recorded, may preserve those real money facts
without paying again or releasing the parcel. Independent reconciliation must
retain the original compensation and actual source journals before onward
physical release. Ordinary recovery completion cannot supply that authority.

After physical pickup, a buyer's refusal of a conforming shipment keeps
outbound shipping spent. An evidenced approved reverse fee belongs to the
buyer only when actual return transport supports it. A prepaid merchandise
refund deducts that reverse fee once, with a zero lower bound; outbound
shipping is not deducted a second time. Unpaid COD has zero buyer refund and
a separately reviewed shipping liability. Excess charges are a separate
reviewed balance, never a negative refund or automatic Wallet debit.
Voluntary post-delivery change of mind remains excluded.

The refusal review retains the actual original failed attempt, its private
proof, the linked destination-hub return receipt, every reverse leg and the
owning seller's final receipt. An independent admin confirms conformity and
the evidenced reverse-cost reference, then approves the displayed original
amounts. Approval moves no money and cannot substitute for those physical
records. Undelivered orders have one exclusive financial authority; a partial
buyer-return loss retains its separate original claim identity.

For an unspent Wallet payment, execution refunds only original net merchandise
less the reverse fee, with a zero lower bound. Original paid outbound shipping
and its funded subsidy go to the outbound shipping pool; the actual withheld
reverse fee goes to a separate reverse-shipping payable pool linked to the
original company. No seller proceeds or merchandise commission are earned by
this unrealized sale. Unused product-voucher commitments are released, while
the actually used outbound shipping subsidy is spent. Neither action restores
the buyer's voucher use automatically. The original quote, payment receipt and
physical return history remain retained.

Uncollected COD has zero buyer repayment and a reviewed shipping liability,
never a fabricated merchandise collection. A buyer's actual cash transfer
retains its private receipt and named receiving admin; only that admin can
acknowledge receipt once. An offer is not received money. For Wallet charges
above the refundable merchandise, a separate buyer-authorized transfer uses
only available credits; it cannot create a negative balance. These receipts
clear the buyer's reviewed obligation without claiming a bank transfer or
automatic carrier payout. Outstanding liability, an unfinished financial
review and unreceived transfers retain account-closure guards.

The web entry is **Return liabilities → Undelivered parcels**. Admins can
open an original returned refusal for review, preview the actual cost and
approve its exact original amounts. The saved preview is scoped to the
current admin and order; refreshing rechecks its original sources. Only cost
fields, amounts and their review token are retained for the preview. Private
files, review reasons and confirmation consent are not cached there.

Approved outcomes link from the buyer's original order and Wallet payment,
seller virtual proceeds and the original admin exception review. The original
buyer can pay a reviewed shipping balance; the named receiving admin confirms
cash receipt. Company owners, including owners with restricted operational
access, and original sellers have their scoped financial summary. They do not
receive the buyer's private transfer receipts, Wallet balance or customer
address through that summary. Review evidence is admin-only; buyer cash
receipts and their acknowledgements remain available to that buyer and
reviewing admins. Uncertain payment failures preserve their original request
identity and direct the participant to saved financial history before retry.

## Privacy and activation

Financial access identifies the original buyer, seller, liable company and
current reviewing admins. Operational floor access does not automatically
grant private funding receipts. Existing liabilities need financial access
even when an operational portal is restricted. Account closure must retain
unreceived transfers and actual outstanding liabilities; a buyer's completed
refund does not clear an advance-funded company or seller debt.

The financial queue is separate from physical return handling. A company
owner sees only its original approved liabilities and financial records,
without the buyer's address, phone, return photos or operational parcel access.
Private source receipts remain with their source account and reviewing admins.
Buyer history retains approval and repayment evidence, while internal funding,
demonstration requests and grant reviews remain private to their participants.

Policy one retains its purchased merchandise-only execution. Unknown versions
require review. Policy two can be issued only after original-unit amounts,
all COD/Wallet funding and settlement consumers, manual loss/refusal outcomes,
privacy, public controls and their acceptance checks are complete. Do not
rewrite old quotes, payments, original delivery evidence or historical refunds.
