# B16 Contract: Scoped Restrictions, Audited Penalties & Review Moderation

## Reconciled Decisions (Topics 11–13, 21, 22, 25 & Topic 27 Debt Controls)
- **Additive scoped enforcement alongside immutable `RestrictionDecision`:** Binary portal locks remain append-only in `restriction_decisions`. B16 adds `enforcement_sanctions` (`ENF-...`) for granular, scoped, reasoned and appealable actions on `buyer`, `seller`/`shop`, and `company`/`logistics_company`:
  - `warning`: formal documented notice with linked evidence; never silently blocks access.
  - `cod_restricted`: blocks Cash-on-Delivery (`cod`) at checkout quote and order placement while preserving Wallet and existing order history/returns.
  - `catalog_visibility_restricted`: hides affected seller/shop products from public marketplace discovery and search (`ShopEligibilityService::availableProducts()`, `BuyerHomeController`, campaign/flash carousels) while preserving order fulfillment, returns, settlement history, and active appeal access. Automatically clears when underlying verified debt settles or when an appeal succeeds.
  - `payout_hold`: holds new seller/courier payout releases until verified debt/penalties are settled or reviewed.
  - `review_restricted`: blocks new product review submissions while preserving order completion, returns, and appeals on removed reviews.
  - `pickup_zone_restricted`: scopes restriction to specific pickup SLA failure evidence without falsely punishing unrelated hubs or paused zones.
- **No negative wallets (`B04` preservation):** `wallet_accounts` remain non-negative at all times. Unpaid penalties, recovery debts, or uncollected fees become `financial_penalties` / obligation ledgers tied to `payout_hold` or `catalog_visibility_restricted` rather than driving wallet balances below zero. When obligations are settled or waived, debt-linked sanctions auto-lift with an immutable audit trail.
- **Non-punitive automated signals (`ModerationSignal`):** Automated chat keyword checks (off-platform bypass indicators, stalled-order warnings, rapid review velocity, weigh-in discrepancies) record non-punitive `moderation_signals` (`SIG-...`) and buyer-facing **Anti-Scam Check-In** prompts. **Keyword detection alone never suspends, bans, or fines an account.**
- **Explicit-authority audited financial penalties (`FinancialPenalty`):** Automated jobs or logistics handlers may only propose `pending_review` records (`PEN-...`) backed by verifiable evidence. Only an independent Admin (never an involved seller/buyer/company owner) may approve (`approved_charged` via non-negative Wallet debit or `unpaid_debt_hold` when balance is insufficient) or waive (`waived`):
  - **Geo-fenced SLA failure (`₱150` default):** Strictly requires verified pickup assignment and absence of `no_service_coverage`, `hub_pickup_pauses`, active `sla_pauses`, `no_available_rider`, or `penalty_exempt`. **Last scan alone never establishes fault.**
  - **Weigh-in tariff discrepancy (`scale_discrepancy_logs`):** Logs declared vs hub scale weight/tariff delta; flags repeated under-declaration patterns and allows audited tariff difference charge without altering immutable order quotes.
  - **Confirmed off-platform bypass / policy breach:** Requires corroborating evidence beyond a chat keyword match.
- **Independent appeals (`EnforcementAppeal` & `ReviewDispute` appeal):** Every scoped sanction and removed review supports a reasoned appeal (`APL-...`) adjudicated by an independent Admin who did not impose the original sanction (`reviewer_id !== issuer_id`), plus automatic expiry and immediate status restoration on `overturned`.
- **Product reviews vs. Courier service feedback:**
  - Verified buyer product reviews require a completed delivered order item, obey a **30-day creation window** from `orders.completed_at` (when recorded), remain blocked while restricted (`review_restricted`), and respect **`BuyerReturnCase` fault rules**: refunded **seller-fault** returns preserve product review eligibility; **courier-fault** or non-seller-fault (`unspecified` / buyer refusal) returns block product review scoring and direct feedback to the courier feedback flow.
  - **Delivery feedback (`delivery_feedbacks`):** Dedicated 1–5 rating and comment per `Delivery` for the courier/logistics company, completely decoupled from `products.rating` and `shops.rating`.
  - **Anti-troll & velocity hold:** Gibberish/repetition or link spam is rejected; sudden rating bursts (`>= 5` reviews in 60 min or `>= 3` 1-star reviews in 15 min on a product) are placed in `held_velocity_review` with a `ModerationSignal` instead of auto-banning buyers.
  - **Complaint preservation without shop score distortion:** Flagging courier keywords or upholding a seller dispute as `upheld_excluded_courier` sets `moderation_status = 'excluded_courier_issue'`, keeping genuine buyer delivery complaints visible with an `Excluded from product score (Delivery issue)` notice while recalculating verified `products.rating` and `shops.rating` from `['published', 'restored']` reviews only. Full removals (`upheld_removed`) allow a buyer appeal that can restore visibility and rating (`restored`).

## Core Invariants
1. **Verified evidence before enforcement:** No scoped restriction (`cod_restricted`, `catalog_visibility_restricted`, `payout_hold`, `review_restricted`, `pickup_zone_restricted`) or financial penalty may be created without `reason_code`, human-readable rationale, and linked evidence or verified order/signal/discrepancy references.
2. **Independent adjudication separation:** The admin deciding an `EnforcementAppeal` or `review_dispute` `appeal_overturned` outcome must never be the party that issued the underlying sanction/removal or an owner of the subject order/shop/company.
3. **Score integrity & public honesty:** Neither `BuyerCampaignController`, `Product`, nor `Shop` may fabricate fallback star ratings (`?: 4.8`), and restricted/closed shops' products stay excluded from homepage flash-sale and discovery feeds.
4. **Wallet & ledger immutability:** Financial penalty collections never mutate historical order totals or create negative `wallet_accounts`; collections happen either via explicit `WalletLedger` debit or tracked `unpaid_debt_hold` obligations cleared on repayment or waiver.
