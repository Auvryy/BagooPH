# Official Marketplace Reference Notes

Checked October 15, 2026. These are public first-party references, not imported
Bagoo policies, branding or software integrations. Availability varies by market
and account. Summaries distinguish product documentation from our own design
recommendations; no internal staffing or review SLA is inferred as a fact.

| Reference | What the source supports | Bagoo interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| [R1: Philippine seller advertising guide](https://ads.shopee.ph/learn/faq/482/1683) | Eligible sellers create shop ads from their merchant tools, set budget/duration and customize creatives. The described shop ads direct buyers to the shop, use click-based charges and can stop at budget/end/manual-stop boundaries. Page states September 7, 2026 update. | Separate seller-supplied marketing from platform content. Reuse the schedule/stop concept; do not import auctions, ad balances, current eligibility thresholds or charges. |
| [R2: Philippine homepage display overview](https://ads.shopee.ph/ad-types/1244) | The public overview describes homepage carousel inventory, advance date reservation, budget/duration/billing setup and banner upload, with impression-based charging. | A homepage image can be commercial inventory, not just a static decoration. For the initial Bagoo release, Platform Admin allocates placements without charging. The page is an overview, not a verified current entitlement or price for every seller. |
| [R3: Official sponsored-display overview](https://www.lazadasolutions.com/solutions-overview/sponsored-display/) | The display solution describes store, product and campaign destinations; homepage/category and other inventory; and access through an eligibility/whitelist process with local commercial or marketing contacts. It notes local inventory differences. | Use meaningful destination types and controlled placement. Do not assume every merchant can upload directly onto a global homepage, or implement every channel listed by the source. |
| [R4: Publicly hosted regional shop-decoration guide](https://cdngarenanow-a.akamaihd.net/shopee/seller/seller_cms/03ceafe9e3aaa921ee5edf96711b644a/%5BMY%5D%20Basic%20Shop%20Decoration%20Guide.pdf) | This older regional guide illustrates seller storefront image/product/category links, preview, draft and publication, separate from marketplace paid placements. | Preserve own-shop branding as an ordinary seller edit. This 2020 document is historical design context, not a current Philippine feature, dimension or eligibility contract. |

The references show different advertising products with different placement,
entry and charging models. They do not establish that every homepage banner is
paid, every banner needs the same review, or every company uses one upload team.

Using existing Platform Admin for editorial ownership, keeping ordinary branding
directly editable, allowing later reviewed seller proposals, selecting three main
and two side images, limiting uploads to 5 MiB, defining internal targets and
delivering AD01-AD04 are explicit Bagoo recommendations. They come from the
project's contracts and inspected code, not requirements imposed by these sources.

Paid search, impression/click billing, offsite advertising, affiliates, video,
live campaigns, new account roles and external analytics remain outside the first
release. No copied screens, third-party visual identity or new commercial service
is proposed.
