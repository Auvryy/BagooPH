# Voucher Design References

Checked October 11, 2026. These public official sources inform interface and
policy choices. They do not define Bagoo's commission, delivery, eligibility
or refund rules. Regional rules vary, so the plan adopts explicit Bagoo
limits rather than claiming every marketplace uses one universal formula.

| Official source | Useful observed pattern | Bagoo interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| [Philippine buyer help: saved vouchers at checkout](https://help.shopee.ph/portal/4/article/85832-How-do-I-use-saved-Vouchers-during-checkout-%28TAG%29) | Saved offers are selectable, with code entry also available. The described checkout supports one platform benefit type and one shop voucher per shop, subject to eligibility. | Choose before typing; visibly separate shop and platform offers. Do not assume three simultaneous voucher types. |
| [Singapore merchant academy: regular seller vouchers](https://seller-sg.tiktok.com/university/essay?default_language=en&knowledge_id=7654184720090881) | Seller funding, minima, discount caps, quantities and per-customer limits are explicit. A saved/claimed offer can become unusable when redemption quota runs out. Seller offers can combine with platform subsidies. | Record payer and limits, explain saving versus redemption, and keep a clear campaign lifecycle. Its cancellation rules are not imported into Bagoo. |
| [Merchant documentation: discount combinations](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/discounts/discount-combinations) | Combination rules distinguish discount classes, calculation order and best eligible savings. | Specify stacking and calculate it centrally; do not copy that product's plan-dependent limits or add another service. |

The funding equation, immutable history, bounded four-batch delivery and
retention of existing cancellation usage are recommendations derived from
Bagoo's inspected services and authoritative contracts. They are not claims
that these sources require Bagoo to use those policies.

No marketplace branding, copied screens, wallet/coins system, live sales
features or external integration is part of the proposal.
